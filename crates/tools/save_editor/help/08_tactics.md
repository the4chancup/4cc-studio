The **Tactics** tab edits the selected player's team's game plan: which of the
three presets is on screen, its formation settings, the advanced instructions
and the team's automatic settings. A field the save's version does not hold
stays in its place, disabled, with a note naming the first version that has it.

The **Preset** buttons pick which of the team's three presets the groups below
edit. Switching presets changes nothing by itself.

**Formation settings** holds the fluid-formation switch (PES 2016 and later),
the five sliders (support range, defensive line, compactness, and the numbers
joining attack and defence) and the seven style pairs, each named as the game
names its two choices.

**Advanced instructions** (PES 2017 and later) sets the team's two attack and
two defence instructions. Tight Marking is never offered: it marks an opponent
and is set during a match, not here; a save already holding it keeps it, and the
row still names it. The **Target** pick is enabled only for the instructions
that take one of the team's own players (Counter Target and Defensive); it
lists the starting eleven by lineup position. Picking an instruction that takes
no target clears the pick.

**Automatic settings** holds the automatic substitution setting as the number
the save stores (0 is off; on PES 2021, 1 is very late, 2 flexible and 3 very
early), the offside trap, the preset change and (PES 2017 and later) the
attack/defence levels.

The two menus above the groups copy or swap whole presets (man marking included)
and the shown preset's three formations; the starting eleven is never touched.
Every change, like every edit in the editor, is one undoable step (Ctrl+Z).
