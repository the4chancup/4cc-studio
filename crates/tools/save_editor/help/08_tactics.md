The **Tactics** tab edits the selected player's team's game plan: which of the
three presets is on screen, its formation settings, the advanced instructions
and the team's automatic settings. A field the save's version does not hold
stays in its place, disabled, with a note naming the first version that has it.

The **pitch** is where the formation takes shape, drawn like the game's own
formation screen: each starter stands at his spot as his portrait over a
badge with his position, his name below it (a name too long for the badge
scrolls now and then, as in the game). The portraits come from the game
picked at the top of the sidebar, out of the `download` folder inside the
**PES folder** setting: with that folder missing, or a file it lists
missing, the starters show without portraits. Dragging a pitch player to a
new place moves him there, snapped to the positions the save can hold. A bench player dropped on a
pitch player swaps them, and a pitch player dropped on a bench row does the
same. Clicking a pitch player opens his details: a position pick, the exact
X and Y fields and **Make GK**, which puts him in goal and brings the
goalkeeper out onto his spot. The goalkeeper cannot be dragged and his
position and spot stay fixed.
The **Kick-off**, **In-possession** and **Out-of-possession** buttons above
the pitch pick which of the preset's three formations it shows (the game
uses the second and third only while the preset's fluid formation is on).
Switching formations changes nothing by itself.

The **Starting eleven** and **Bench** lists set who plays and in which order:
drag a row onto another to move or swap it. A starter dropped on
another starter trades their places. A bench player dropped on a starter takes
his place and every job he held (set pieces, joining the attack, instruction
targets); the captain stays who he was.

The **Player assignments** chips set who takes each set piece, the captain and
who joins the attack: click a chip, then click the pitch player. Join attack
keeps its mode open so up to three can be picked or unpicked; Done finishes.

The **Preset** buttons at the top, beside the two Copy/swap menus, pick which
of the team's three presets the groups below edit. Switching presets changes
nothing by itself.

**Formation settings** holds the fluid-formation switch (PES 2016 and later),
the five sliders (support range, defensive line, compactness, and the numbers
joining attack and defence) and the seven style pairs, each named as the game
names its two choices.

**Advanced instructions** (PES 2017 and later) sets the team's two attack and
two defence instructions. Tight Marking is never offered: it marks an opponent
and is set during a match, not here; a save already holding it keeps it, and the
row still names it. The **Target** pick is enabled only for the instructions
that take one of the team's own players (Counter Target and Defensive); it
lists the starting eleven by lineup position. Picking an instruction that takes
no target clears the pick.

**Automatic settings** holds the automatic substitution setting as the number
the save stores (0 is off; on PES 2021, 1 is very late, 2 flexible and 3 very
early), the offside trap, the preset change and (PES 2017 and later) the
attack/defence levels.

The two menus above the groups copy or swap whole presets (man marking included)
and the shown preset's three formations; the starting eleven is never touched.
Every change, like every edit in the editor, is one undoable step (Ctrl+Z).
